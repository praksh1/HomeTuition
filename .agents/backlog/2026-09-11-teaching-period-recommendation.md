# Teaching-period recommendation — awaiting owner approval

Research date: 2026-09-11. Product recommendation, NOT an approved billing change.

## Public first-party evidence

- https://www.midaseclass.com/eclass1 advertises tuition/help/exam preparation in 1/3/6/12-month
  packages. It does not establish that all monthly renewals occur on the first of a BS month.
- https://www.midaseclass.com/midasforschools describes a 20-class hobby package with variable
  weekly attendance, supporting lesson-bundle versus ongoing-tuition differentiation.
- https://helps.mysecondteacher.com.np/general describes one/six/twelve-month packages and buying
  access through local payment methods. Its public page does not establish the calendar anchoring
  required to copy a renewal rule. Some source content is older; no prices adopted as current quotes.
- https://serviceapi.docs.khalti.com/Internet/subisu/ shows a sample 30-day service validity, but this
  is internet billing, NOT evidence of a Nepal tuition industry standard.

No verified universal Nepali education-app rule choosing BS calendar months versus rolling periods
was found in these pages. Do not claim one exists based on package labels alone.

## Codex recommendation

Initial ongoing tuition: shared 30-day teaching periods anchored to the group's start, not each
student's payment date. Show BS and AD dates, exact lessons, full advance price and next renewal
before purchase. Clearly label every 30 days, not a calendar month. Preserve the same group across
renewals. Fixed courses: one dated lesson bundle paid before start, not forced into monthly billing.
This fits the existing 30-day machinery but must not inherit legacy daily/25-held/5-makeup rules
without an explicit owner decision. Thirty days is not thirty lessons.

Teacher-permitted mid-period entry could charge only eligible remaining lessons with a clear
partial-period quote and the same group renewal date; this is proposed future work, not shipped
or approved proration logic. No silent auto-debit or unpaid-credit access is implied by renewal.
BS-calendar billing can be added later if demand warrants it; do not implement both calendars as
separate billing engines for the first release.

Next step: owner accepts or changes the recommended period before implementation. Neither real
money nor a new renewal system is enabled by this research.
