# Classroom follow-up checks

Live on production: **9e2c418**, 24 September 2026, after the full production gate passed. Detailed evidence: `.agents/worklog/2026-09-24-codex-classroom-followups.md`.

1. **Teacher, phone:** open Participants. Search a student; use Hands to bring the raised-hand queue into view. The panel should fill the usable screen, its list should scroll, and Permissions should remain reachable with the keyboard open.
2. **Teacher, whiteboard:** open Board pages → Clear this page. The confirmation names exactly what is removed. Cancel should change nothing. Confirm should clear only this whiteboard page for everyone; Undo should restore drawings and imported material for teacher and student.
3. **Student, phone and laptop:** the small people button beside the lesson title counts connected students including you, not the teacher. Tap it to see classmates. It is a presence count, not an attendance grade or total enrollment.
4. **Two student accounts in the same class:** tap a classmate's message icon, send privately, and close the panel. The classroom should stay open. Replies and the full thread are in Messages. Teachers do not receive these private messages.
5. **Messaging safety:** in a private conversation, Safety & help → Block. Neither person should be able to send new messages or reactions to the other; existing messages remain. Only the person who created that block can remove it. Report this conversation starts a support request; it does not automatically ban anyone.

Automated testing covers larger rosters and phone/desktop browser sizes. Real iPhone/Android browser and keyboard behavior still benefits from the owner's device testing. No payment rules, LiveKit media permissions or refund entitlements changed.
